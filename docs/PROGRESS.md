## Log

- **Mon 12 Oct** — Architecture chosen: layered modular monolith with the guard as the middle layer ([docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md)).
- **Mon 12 Oct** — M0: backend and sandbox boot and report healthy; `.env` generated with random secrets; CI workflow (Ubuntu 3.12, Windows 3.13); 47 tests pass, including the architecture rules that keep the agent layer away from executors, the DB and the signing key.
- **Mon 12 Oct** — M1: the sandbox provider (wallet, bank, messaging, lender) refuses any side effect without a valid guard signature bound to the exact payee, amount and idempotency key; retries pay once; frozen accounts refuse payments; every request lands in the operation log. The generator builds 400 shops plus Rahman Store (8,657 ledger entries, 345 days). `python scripts/seed.py` loads the books into the backend and opens matching accounts in the sandbox. 87 tests pass.
- **Mon 12 Oct** — M2: the guard is complete and deterministic, with no LLM code yet: provenance, kill switch, PIN lock, hidden-instruction scan (English, Banglish, Bangla, homoglyphs, zero-width, base64), exact owner-intent matching, payee integrity, limits, split and velocity detection, fraud score; tiers 0–3 and Blocked; approvals with argon2id PIN and 15-minute lockout; kill switch that also freezes the provider; scheduled payments re-checked on their day; guard-signed tokens verified by the provider; hash-chained append-only audit log. `python scripts/guard_walkthrough.py` plays 8 scenes on the demo shop. 199 tests pass.
- **Tue 13 Oct** — M3: the agent works end to end through `/api/chat`. Claude (Sonnet 5.5, adaptive thinking, append-only turns, refusal fallback), any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or a deterministic and deliberately gullible mock. Bounded loop; every tool call goes through the guard as its own transaction. The bookkeeper books typed sales, purchases, baki and expenses (amounts must be in the owner's words) and forwarded bKash/Nagad/bank SMS by reference (a customer's payment is matched to their baki; a payment to a supplier settles their bill; duplicates refused). Quarantined parser with no tools; "stop" and PIN-like messages handled before any model. 273 tests pass (incl. 9 end-to-end chat scenarios); 4 live Claude tests are opt-in (`pytest -m live`). Fraud Sentinel's models can't score mobile-money payments (they need card-dataset PCA features), so M4 ports its design instead (DECISIONS #39).
- **Tue 13 Oct** — M4: Fraud Sentinel, mobile-money edition, scores every proposed payment (Isolation Forest + calibrated LightGBM, amount-aware thresholds, plain-English reasons, integrity-checked model file, rule-based fallback). On 50,090 synthetic payments (chronological hold-out): ROC-AUC 0.993, 77% of fraud flagged at 1.1% false alarms vs 43% for the rules, banking risk cost down 54%; blind spots reported honestly. Supplier payer: known-but-untrusted payees resolved from the books (still PIN), background scheduler with owner notifications, demo endpoints to advance simulated time and run due payments. 10 new end-to-end payment scenarios (new payee, spoof, splitting, PIN lockout, invoice injection, STOP pausing a scheduled payment until resumed with the PIN, double approval pays once, Banglish, limits not changeable from chat, owner raising the limit with the PIN). 294 tests pass.
- **Tue 13 Oct** — M5: cash forecast. A 14-day day-by-day projection from explainable parts (weekday baselines adjusted for Ramadan/Eid, open and recurring bills, supplier credit and each regular supplier's next bill, scheduled payments netted against the bills they pay, baki coming back as a flow) with an 80% range, the first shortfall day and its cause, and suggestions re-simulated to show their effect (chase overdue baki, ask a supplier to wait, hold back withdrawals, a short loan). On the demo shop it warns a week ahead: Tuesday 20 Oct, when Meghna's ৳42,175 falls due. Ask in chat, `GET /api/forecast`, or let the daily check alert the owner once. Backtest on 6,239 windows across 401 synthetic shops: 14-day money-in median error 6.5% vs 6.9% and 8.7% for naive forecasts; the 80% band covers 86%. 312 tests pass.
- **Tue 13 Oct** — M6: credit profile and loans. With the owner's consent (given and withdrawn only in the app; no agent tool can touch it), the books become a band A–E, a probability of default and the top SHAP reasons in plain words. One feature function serves training and the app (a test proves the demo shop's features match its training data exactly). Monotone LightGBM with Platt calibration on 400 synthetic shops: out-of-fold ROC-AUC 0.729 (the hidden true probability scores 0.779; logistic regression 0.690), calibration error 3.4%. Shop type and region are never inputs; sales level, margin and mobile-money share are shown but not scored, because scoring them added no accuracy and widened the urban/rural good-payer gap from 11 to 15 points. Fairness report with intervals in docs/results/credit_model.md. Four illustrative loan products matched on eligibility, cost and affordability; the pre-filled application is held for the owner's approval and sent to the sandbox lender with a token bound to its exact contents. The demo shop scores band B, and "can I get a loan for Eid stock?" recommends a ৳30,000 microfinance loan at ৳5,356 a month. Data export and consent withdrawal included. 345 tests pass.

- **Tue 13 Oct** — M8: red-team suite (`redteam/`). 108 attacks in 18 categories and 38 benign look-alikes, each run in a fresh copy of the demo shop with its accounts funded far beyond any attack, through the real doors (owner chat with forwarded SMS and invoices, other people's messages, the owner API, the provider API), with the model gullible, *fooled* (the attacker's tool calls added) or *compromised* (only the attacker's calls). Outcomes are checked from outside: money that actually left the provider, limits/payees/consent/STOP, submissions to the lender, the PIN anywhere in responses, history, audit log, trace or provider log, and no shop data for strangers. Every case is a pytest test in CI, which also uploads the report. Live mode (`--mode anthropic`) is built; the live run waits for an API key (M10). 525 tests pass (DECISIONS #64–71).
